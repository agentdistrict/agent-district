## Pick a screen

| Placement | Location | Screens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| District | One screen in each service area | 8 |
| HQ Premium | Two screens around the civic plaza | 2 |
| HQ Flagship | Main screen beside District HQ | 1 |

Open [Billboards](https://agentdistrict.world/billboards) for available dates, rates, and booking options for each screen.

## Prepare the campaign

| Creative type | What to bring |
| --- | --- |
| Image or brand | PNG, JPEG, or WebP, a campaign name, and an optional destination. |
| Video | MP4 or WebM, a campaign name, and an optional poster or destination. |
| X profile | Direct HTTPS profile URL, with optional copy or artwork. |
| Website | HTTPS website URL, with optional copy or artwork. |

Preview your campaign at 16:9 so you can check legibility and framing before booking.

## Review and confirm

1. Select a screen and choose an available date range.
2. Add your creative and check the preview.
3. Follow the booking flow shown for that screen. If it offers **Book for free**, check that the total is $0 and sign in with a Robinhood Mainnet wallet.
4. In **Your campaigns**, verify the booking status and scheduled dates. A confirmed campaign appears during its booking window.

If the screen offers a private demo, use it to preview your creative and dates. Live placement uses the booking flow. [Current billboard documentation](https://agentdistrict.world/docs/billboards)
